Saturday 22 May 2027. Please keep the afternoon free until about 7:00 PM.

The ceremony

Twenty minutes in the old council chamber at Corrisham Town Hall. Please be seated by 11:45 AM.

Lunch in the garden

A marquee on the lawn at Lantry Mead, a ten-minute walk down Bridle Row from the town hall.

What to wear

Garden smart. Flat shoes help on the lawn, and a light layer for later.

Getting there

Park once in the Wharf Lane car park. Both places are a short walk from it.

Reply

One form for each invitation, with a lunch choice for every guest named on it.

How to reply

One reply for each invitation. If anything changes, just send it again.

  1. Add your namesExactly as they appear on the card, so we can tick you off.
  2. Say yes or noIf you can't come, we will miss you, and there is no need to explain.
  3. Choose lunchOne choice for each guest, plus anything the kitchen should know.
  4. Look out for a noteWe send the final times and the taxi number in early May.

Questions

What should I wear?

Garden smart. Lunch is on a lawn, so heels that sink are best left at home.

Can I bring a plus-one?

If your invitation names a guest, yes. Please add their name on the reply.

Are children invited?

Yes, if they are named on the card. There is a children's plate and a box of lawn games.

Where do I park?

Wharf Lane car park, which is free on Saturdays. Both places are a short walk from it.

What if it rains?

The marquee has sides, and we will have umbrellas for the walk down Bridle Row.

Do you have a gift list?

No gift list. Your company is plenty. If you would like to give something, a card with a memory in it would make us very happy.

Finding us in Corrisham

The town hall sits on Market Square. Lantry Mead is at the bottom of Bridle Row, by the river path.
